[SECTION: GLOSSARY]
•
HIFU: High Intensity Focused Ultrasound is a non-invasive therapeutic technique that uses non-ionizing
ultrasonic waves to heat or ablate tissue.
•
Venous reflux or Chronic Venous Insufficiency (CVI): occurs when veins no longer circulate blood
properly. It often affects the leg veins, causing blood to pool and leading to swelling and discomfort.
•
Serious Adverse Events (SAE): an adverse reaction that results in death, is life-threatening, requires
hospitalisation or prolongation of existing hospitalisation, results in persistent or significant disability
or incapacity, or is a birth defect.
•
CEAP classification: the CEAP (Clinical-Etiology-Anatomy-Pathophysiology) classification is an
internationally accepted standard for describing patients with chronic venous disorders
