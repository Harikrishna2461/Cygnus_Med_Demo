Closed Shunt
Pages: ~100-103
  - Definition: "Closed circuit" recirculation
  - Mechanism: Diastolic activation
  - Pressure gradient: Hydrostatic column
  - Clinical significance

Open Deviated Shunt
Pages: ~103-106
  - Definition: Deviation without recirculation
  - No return to origin
  - Diastolic activation only
  - Minimal clinical significance

Open Bypassing Shunt (Vicarious)
Pages: ~106-110
  - Definition: Bypasses venous obstruction
  - Escape point distal to obstruction
  - Re-entry proximal to obstruction
  - Systolic activation (mostly)
  - Indicates significant pathology

SPECIFIC ESCAPE POINT SECTIONS:

Inguinal Point (IP)
Pages: ~160-165
  - Location: 2 cm above saphenous arch
  - Embryological basis: ductus Nuck reopening
  - Feeding vein: round ligament vein
  - Creates: closed shunts 4 and 5
  - Diagnostic: color Doppler in standing position

Perineal Point (PP)
Pages: ~165-170
  - Location: superficial perineal fascia perforation
  - Anatomical basis: perineal vein escape
  - Feeding: labial veins, internal pudendal vein
  - Creates: closed shunts 4 and 5
  - Diagnosis: gynecologic position preferred for Duplex

Superior Gluteal Escape Point (SGEP)
Pages: ~170-172
  - Location: mid-buttock
  - Creates: closed shunt 3 or 5
  - Small saphenous system involvement

Inferior Gluteal Escape Point (IGEP)
Pages: ~172-174
  - Location: inferior buttock or popliteal fossa
  - Particularly common in CVM
  - Creates: varices around sciatic nerve

Obturator Escape Point (OP)
Pages: ~174-175
  - Location: obturator foramen
  - Usually hemodynamically controlled
  - Can create: closed shunts 4 or 5 if variant anatomy

Congenital Venous Malformations (CVM)
