Fascia

In this medical ultrasound image captured with a high-frequency linear transducer for varicose vein or vascular examination, please identify and precisely segment the superficial fascial plane. The fascia appears as a thin, continuous, hyperechoic (bright white) connective-tissue sheet that courses nearly horizontally across the full width of the ultrasound field, forming the dominant bright interface separating subcutaneous adipose tissue above from deeper muscular and vascular compartments below…

Vein

In this medical ultrasound image captured with a high-frequency linear transducer for varicose vein or vascular examination, please identify and precisely segment all visible subcutaneous veins and venous blood vessels. Subcutaneous veins appear as anechoic (dark/black) compressible tubular structures with thin hyperechoic walls — round or oval in cross-section, or two parallel bright walls in longitudinal view. Veins of primary interest (CHIVA protocol) include the GSV, SSV, and superficial tributaries…
